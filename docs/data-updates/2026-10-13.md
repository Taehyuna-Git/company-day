# Company information update — 2026-10-13

80 companies received reviewed additions: 20 official founding dates, 81 purpose-labelled public email contacts, one explicitly identified representative email, five head-office telephone numbers, and one official website. `2026-10-13.json` records each field, previous value, new value and primary source.

Official-page research covered the 2,652 registered companies. Pages were retrieved for 1,759; 893 had no registered site or could not be retrieved. Retrieval does not mean every field was verified. Earlier October 9 and October 12 page captures were reviewed alongside current official sources; only manually accepted candidates were promoted.

## Dates

`anniversary` retains the company's official founding date. `founded` retains legal incorporation and is not overwritten. `commemoration()` selects the official anniversary first, otherwise a valid incorporation date, explicitly labelled `설립기념일 · 법인 설립일 기준`. Existing legal dates have not been relabelled as newly researched founding dates. There are 34 official anniversaries, 2,605 incorporation-based dates and 13 companies without either date.

Shinhan Alpha REIT's 2017-12-18 is an incorporation-based event. Cosmecca Korea separately shows legal incorporation 1999-10-05 and official founding 2000-05-12. Some companies use predecessor history; their legal date and a visible explanatory note are preserved.

Purpose-specific contacts remain separate from representative email. Privacy contacts, unclear company identity and incomplete anniversary dates remain held. Unknown fields continue to say `정보 수집 중`.
